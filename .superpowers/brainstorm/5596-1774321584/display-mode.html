How should upcoming holidays appear?

The card currently shows the weekly Shabbat countdown. How should holiday info fit in?

A

Inline Enhancement

Add holiday info to the existing card — e.g., a small line below the Shabbat info showing "Pesach in 5 days". The card stays one unified view, holiday info appears when relevant and disappears when not.

B

Mode Switching

The card switches its primary display when a holiday is close enough (e.g., within 7 days). Instead of "Shabbat Shalom / Until Candle Lighting", it shows the holiday countdown as the main content. Shabbat info moves to secondary or is suppressed if the holiday is imminent.

C

Dual Display

The card always shows both — Shabbat info on one side, upcoming holiday on the other. Could be a split layout or stacked sections. More information density but larger card footprint.
