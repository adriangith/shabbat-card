When should the card switch to holiday mode?

How far in advance should the holiday take over as the primary display?

A

Fixed: Always 7 days before

Simple and predictable. One week out, the card switches. Same threshold for all holidays — Pesach, Chanukah, Yom Kippur, fast days.

B

Tiered by holiday type

Major Yom Tov (Pesach, RH, etc.) switches 14 days out — there's real preparation needed. Minor holidays and fast days switch 3–5 days out — just a heads up.

C

User configurable

Add a setting in the card editor: "Switch to holiday mode X days before". Default to 7, but the user can adjust per their needs.

D

Gradual: badge first, then switch

Combine approaches — show a small inline badge starting 14 days out, then fully switch to holiday mode at 3 days. Best of both worlds but more complex.
