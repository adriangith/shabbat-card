Pick your priority model:

A

Shabbat always wins when imminent

Once we're within ~24h of candle lighting, the card reverts to Shabbat mode (candle, שבת שלום, Shabbat countdown). The holiday info drops to a small secondary line: "Pesach in 2 days". After Havdalah, holiday mode resumes.

B

Holiday stays primary, Shabbat merges in

The card stays in Pesach mode but adds the Shabbat times: "Candle Lighting 6:03 PM · Havdalah 6:59 PM". The visual identity stays Pesach. Shabbat info is contextual, not a mode switch.

C

Closest event always wins

Pure proximity logic. Whichever is closer (Shabbat candle lighting vs. holiday start) takes over the card. Simple rule, no special cases. During active Shabbat the card always shows Shabbat; after Havdalah it flips back to the holiday countdown.
